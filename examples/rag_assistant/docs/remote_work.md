# Remote work policy

## Eligibility
All employees who completed their trial period can work remotely up to three days per week.
Teams agree on one shared office day so that everyone meets in person at least once a week.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year, after approval by
HR. Some countries are excluded for tax and data protection reasons; HR keeps the list.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monitor. A home-office allowance of 25 EUR per month covers
internet and electricity.
