# Expense policy

## What can be expensed
Travel for client meetings, conference tickets approved by your manager, and work equipment
under 300 EUR can be expensed. Meals during business travel are covered up to 35 EUR per day.

## How to submit an expense
Submit receipts in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Each expense needs a
photo of the receipt and the project code. Expenses above 1,000 EUR need written approval from
your manager before the purchase.

## Reimbursement
Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly salary payment. Late submissions (after
30 days) are reviewed case by case by the finance team.
