# Security incident reporting

## What is a security incident
A lost or stolen laptop or phone, a suspicious email you clicked on, a password you shared by
mistake, or customer data sent to the wrong person are all security incidents.

## How to report an incident
Report it immediately, even outside working hours, to the security team on the #security-alerts
channel or by phone on the on-call number in the intranet. Do not try to investigate alone and
do not delete anything: evidence matters.

## Personal data breaches
If personal data is involved, the data protection officer must be informed within 24 hours.
Under GDPR the company has 72 hours to notify the supervisory authority (the CNIL in France).
