# Onboarding

## First day
New employees receive their laptop and badge from IT at 9:00 on their first day. Your manager
introduces you to the team and assigns an onboarding buddy for the first month.

## First week
Complete the mandatory security awareness training and the GDPR training in the learning
portal during your first week. Access to production systems is granted only after both
trainings are completed.

## Trial period
The trial period lasts four months for engineers and three months for other roles. A review
meeting with your manager is scheduled halfway through.
